# Pictures and diagrams

Sometimes a picture explains a topic faster than any paragraph or ASCII diagram. Then it has to be
**made**, not described. This reference covers when to draw, how, where to put it and, most of all,
**how to check** that the picture does not lie.

## When to draw and when not

A picture is justified only if it **adds understanding** the text and ASCII do not give. A note
without pictures is not worse; a useless picture is.

**Draw** when the point cannot be put in text:

- **color**: a color wheel, a spectrum, light temperature;
- **2D geometry**: light placement on a set, angles, a spatial layout;
- **curves and graphs**: a retention curve, an ADSR envelope, a compressor transfer curve;
- **gradients and overlap**: soft shadow, falloff, overlapping frequency bands;
- **shape**: a standing wave on a string, synth waveforms;
- **before/after side by side**, when the visual difference is the point.

**Do not draw** (keep ASCII or a table) when those cope:

- chains of processes and states (`A --> B --> C`);
- timelines, simple trees and hierarchies;
- comparison by attributes: that is a table.

> One question decides it: **"what will the reader get from the picture that they would not get
> from ASCII?"** No clear answer: no picture.

There is one managed exception: when the structure **fits a ready template** (SWOT, funnel,
roadmap, comparison of two options) and a legible presentation picture is wanted, see "Template
infographics and data charts" below.

## Format: SVG, not raster

**SVG only.** It renders natively in Obsidian, scales with any font size, works offline and holds
exactly what was drawn. Downloading other people's images into the vault brings licensing issues,
link rot and a dependency on the network.

The picture is **generated by code** (Python), not written by hand, so it is easy to fix and rebuild.

## How

1. **A generator script** builds the SVG from strings and writes the file. Keep a `txt(x, y, ...)`
   helper for labels and one shared palette; do not hardcode colors in place.
2. **The file** goes into `Diagrams/` next to the topic (`Media/Diagrams/color-wheel.svg`).
3. **Embed** it where the ASCII would have been: `![[color-wheel.svg]]`, usually right after the
   paragraph it illustrates.
4. **Keep the generator** next to the diagrams (`Diagrams/_generator-N.py`), so the picture can be
   rebuilt. It is the picture's source, not litter.

Do not write into Obsidian's attachments folder; diagrams live in `<Topic>/Diagrams/`.

## Style

So the diagrams look like one system:

- **An own light background** (e.g. `#faf9f7`) with rounded corners. Not transparent: Obsidian's dark
  theme would make dark text invisible.
- **One palette**: dark ink for text (`#23211e`), muted grey for notes (`#6b6660`), light grey for
  grid lines (`#c9c4bc`). Accents by meaning (green good, red bad or tension, blue and ochre for
  neutral categories), the same across the topic.
- **Large type**: title about 22, labels 11-15.
- **Title + subtitle** on top and **a one-line conclusion** at the bottom: the picture must read on its own.
- **Sans-serif** via `font-family` (Inter / Segoe UI / Helvetica / Arial).

## Checking: a required step

**No diagram is embedded unchecked.** Generate it, render to PNG, **look at it**. A defect turns up
almost every time.

Render with the headless Chrome or Edge already installed:

```bash
# 1. build a preview page with the SVGs inlined (not linked: links do not survive the snapshot)
# 2. screenshot it headless
chrome --headless --disable-gpu --hide-scrollbars --screenshot=check.png --window-size=940,1200 file:///path/preview.html
# 3. open check.png with the Read tool and inspect it
```

What to check, **in order of importance**:

1. **The diagram does not lie about the content.** More important than layout. If the label says
   "the curve crosses the ceiling", the curve must actually cross it, or the picture teaches the
   opposite of the text next to it. Take the data from the same verified facts as the text, and
   **compute** where needed (a phase, a frequency) instead of adjusting by eye.
2. **Nothing is clipped or outside the canvas**: labels at the edges and the last item in a row are
   the usual victims. Widen the `viewBox` or shift.
3. **Labels do not overlap** each other or the shapes.

Found a defect: **fix the generator and render again** until clean; usually 1-3 passes.

## Template infographics and data charts: the `graphics` skill

For **discrete structures** (lists, steps, comparisons, SWOT, funnel, roadmap, hierarchy) and
**charts from real data**, do not hand-draw an SVG: the `graphics` skill covers them offline (a
vendored AntV Infographic build and its own chart generators). This section's hand-made generators
stay for analogue things (color, curves, geometry, shape).

Storage, embedding and the required render check are the same as for hand-made diagrams.

## After embedding

- Check that every `![[*.svg]]` points at an existing file (the link checker covers embeds).
- **No cards from a diagram**: cards ask about facts, not pictures.
